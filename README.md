# PSY2124 Cognitive Psychology
## Serial Position Experiment

Explore how a word’s position in a list relates to your recall.

1. Open the experiment webpage and choose **1–5 trials**, then select **Start Test**.
2. Watch 15 words appear, one at a time. Try to remember them without taking notes.
3. Enter the words you remember, separated by spaces, commas, or line breaks. Order does not matter.
4. Submit your recall after each trial, then select **Next Trial** when ready. After the final trial, select **View Results** to see combined recall counts by position.

Each session uses different lists in random order, with no repeats. Words within each list are also shuffled. The graph counts recall at each position across trials (0–number of trials).

**Offline use:** Select **Download the experiment for offline use** on the webpage. Save `serial-recall-download.html` and open it in a browser; no internet connection is needed after downloading.

**Reflect:** Did you remember more words from the beginning, middle, or end? One attempt may not show the typical primacy and recency effects.

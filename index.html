    Understanding Your Graph:
    • Primacy: Better recall of early items is often associated with greater opportunity for rehearsal and long-term memory encoding.
    • Middle positions: Recall is often lower for middle items. A single attempt may not show this pattern.
    • Recency: Better recall of late items in immediate free recall is often associated with their continued short-term availability.
    • Interpretation: A smooth serial-position probability curve requires observations across participants or trials. Your results need not be U-shaped.
